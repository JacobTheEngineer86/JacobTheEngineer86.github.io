title: Learning Chronicles
---
I’ve read a ton of books, listened to quite a few podcasts, watched a lot of thought provoking videos (thanks TED), read plenty of articles, and generally consumed a fair amount of high quality content. That’s great, but I need a place to capture and record what I’ve learned. If I don’t, the lessons are more likely to be forgotten, and the time I spent consuming the information will not have been as productive as it could’ve been. 

This is my space to capture those lessons and ideas. I will extract all I can from the different sources, even if I don’t agree with the ideas being presented. My goal is for this to be a somewhat unbiased collection of knowledge that focuses on what the creator is trying to say, regardless of my own beliefs and interpretations. That said, I’m absolutely going to point to my own thoughts in my [Synthesis of Ideas](Synthesis%20of%20Ideas) section. 

It’s important to separate my own ideas from someone else’s ideas so that I can dig into and fully understand what they’re saying without my own biases getting in the way. By doing that, it will be significantly easier to recognize where I’m wrong, then I can change my beliefs to what makes the most sense to me.
