I love *living!* There’s something truly special about being alive. As explained in [[The Power of Now]], living insanely in the present moment is a gift that brings such amazing opportunities. Why do people like skydiving, sports, art, hanging out with friends, etc.? Because it brings them into the present moment and shows the joy of really living. 

I have a lot of hobbies, which gives me a lot of opportunities to live in the present moment. Each hobby has the potential to bring me into the present moment in a very unique way. It can be easy to get stuck in a rut while doing some of these hobbies - becoming so familiar with it that the hobby becomes routine, that it disengages the mind. 

The ideal scenario with hobbies is that there’s a continuous push for growth and improvement. When one thing becomes second nature, you continue to build up, to work on a new aspect of the hobby. Then that becomes second nature and you find something else to improve, and the process continues - A spiral of continuous improvement. Then one day you realize that you’ve actually improved quite a lot - not all at once, but over the course of years.
