
“A man who dares to waste one hour of time has not discovered the value of life.” 
— Charles Darwin

If this quote makes you feel guilty for wasting time, you’re reading it wrong. 

The key to understanding this quote lies in truly understanding what it means to waste time. Relaxation is not (necessarily) wasting time. Taking breaks is not (necessarily) wasting time. 

What is wasting time is procrastination. 

Relaxation is crucial for living a high quality life. So is working hard. You must have both. But living only in a state of one or the other leads to a low quality life. 

Alive-time versus dead-time 
— Robert Greene
