## The State of Consciousness

One major point of discussion that affects all belief systems is the state of consciousness - does consciousness precede our current mortal experience, or is our current mortal experience simply the result of chemical reactions happening in our brains?

Which came first - mind or matter?

## “Scientific” Materialism

According to the scientific materialistic point of view, consciousness begins at or near birth, when the physical body is constructed enough to the point that reality can be experienced.

## Finding Common Ground

The division between these belief systems is thinner than is often thought. There’s much more common ground than there is division. That’s not to say there’s no division, there absolutely is - put a Christian and an Atheist in a room together and listen to the conflicting ideas of the state of consciousness, of the existence of a higher power, of our ability to understand the true reality of the universe. 

But even with these massive differences of belief, we can and should learn to live with and respect the belief systems of the people around us. To think that someone is in any way “lesser” than we are because they believe different than we do is to close our eyes to the humanity around us - it is to refuse to learn and grow from our fellow humans. 

That’s different from changing your beliefs. You can and should hold fast to your belief system. And you should strive to understand other belief systems, which makes your belief system even stronger. 

You should test your belief system, question it, make it stronger. You cannot rely on someone else’s belief system - you must get to the roots, build, and fortify your own belief system.

# Free Will

Do we have free will? Do we make choices as a result of some force within us? Or are our choices already predetermined, set in stone by our environment and our upbringing?

To answer these questions, we must have an understanding about the core nature of consciousness. There are two options here – either scientific materialism is true, or it’s not. 

If scientific materialism is true, and our conscious reality is strictly a result of the chemical reactions in our brain and the environment surrounding us - This leaves the door open for the idea that everything is predetermined, that free will does not exist.

If scientific materialism is false, and there is some part of our consciousness that is external to / apart from the chemical reactions in our brain (soul, intelligence, etc.) - This leaves the door open for the idea that we have free will. The decisions we make originate from that external part of our consciousness.
